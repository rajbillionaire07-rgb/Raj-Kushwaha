Place your edited video files here (e.g. reel_careerguide.mp4, bitsat_promo.mp4, showreel_2025.mp4).

Then in src/data/portfolioData.ts, reference them like this:
videoUrl: '/videos/reel_careerguide.mp4'

Supported formats:
- MP4 (H.264 / AAC) - Recommended for best cross-browser compatibility
- WebM
- YouTube / YouTube Shorts links (e.g. https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YOUR_ID or https://youtu.be/YOUR_ID)
- Vimeo links
